# Integration contract

The Berlin Urban Heat Twin exposes versioned JSON structures for downstream Berlin digital-twin projects. OpenAPI is generated by FastAPI and validated in CI.

## HeatSnapshot v1.0

`HeatSnapshot` contains:

- timezone-aware `timestamp`;
- interchange `crs` (`EPSG:4326`);
- `state` with counts, derived indicators, quality and limitations;
- active `stations` with provenance;
- latest `observations` with explicit unit-bearing field names and provenance;
- cached `official_areas` with source/layer identity and untouched attributes;
- `freshness_hours`;
- `spatial_coverage`;
- `uncertainty`.

Downstream consumers must not interpret the derived station median as an interpolated city surface.

## ThermalArea v1.0

`ThermalArea` is the stable area-oriented contract and contains:

- timezone-aware `timestamp`;
- `area_id`;
- GeoJSON `geometry` and `crs`;
- typed `ThermalIndicator[]`, each carrying value, unit, state type, method and provenance;
- area-level `state_type` and `source`;
- optional `official_classification`;
- `provenance`;
- optional `freshness_hours`;
- `quality` and `uncertainty`.

The contract intentionally prevents a numeric indicator from being passed downstream without a unit/state/provenance context.

## Analytical contracts

`AreaSummary`, `NumericAttributeSummary`, `GroupedAreaSummary` and `StationClimateOverlap` expose methodology and uncertainty alongside results. These are derived analytical outputs, not replacements for the original source layers.

## Cross-project use

- **Resilience Twin:** consume official affected polygons, area summaries and provenance; overlay infrastructure/critical assets in its own domain.
- **Energy Twin:** use measured station temperature/humidity with timestamps/quality and optionally documented official climate attributes as contextual covariates.
- **Environmental/Mobility Twins:** use spatial climate queries or official-area intersections without importing a false live temperature surface.

Cross-domain causal claims require separate evidence and are not implied by spatial overlap alone.
